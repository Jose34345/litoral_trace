# Litoral Trace V1 — Controlled Pilot / Design Partner

## Purpose

A 30-day controlled implementation used to validate Litoral Trace with a real operating workflow before the customer treats the platform as a production system of record.

## Recommended commercial offer

Reference one-time pilot fee: USD 500–1,000 depending on onboarding effort, source-data cleanup, document volume and training required.

After a successful pilot, the recommended conversion is to the applicable recurring plan, normally Compliance at USD 499/month for the initial target segment.

## Pilot scope

- 1 customer organization
- 1–3 initial users
- approximately 20–500 initial lots unless otherwise agreed
- import of one representative XLSX workflow
- lot/geolocation review
- representative Vault evidence documents
- representative satellite analyses
- audit/evidence walkthrough
- one onboarding session
- one closeout review and go/no-go recommendation

## Customer inputs

The customer provides source XLSX files, lot/geolocation information, evidence documents, designated pilot users, and one business owner able to validate whether the workflow reflects current operations.

## Controlled-pilot boundaries

During the pilot:

- the customer retains its original source records and documents
- Litoral Trace is not the customer's sole system of record
- there is no production SLA unless separately contracted
- no legal certification or guaranteed EUDR compliance is implied
- integrations with ERP/mission-critical systems are excluded unless explicitly scoped
- production cutover requires the production-readiness gates and a separate go-live decision

## Success criteria

The pilot is successful when the customer can reconstruct a representative chain from lot/origin data through geolocation, supporting documents, satellite evidence where applicable, and the corresponding audit/evidence trail without reverting to an unmanaged collection of spreadsheets, email and folders.

Commercial validation is separate: the pilot must also produce a named owner, an agreed next step, and a clear decision on paid continuation.

## Exit

At closeout, choose one outcome:

1. convert to paid production plan;
2. extend the controlled pilot under a written extension; or
3. stop and follow the agreed export/offboarding procedure.

# U.S. Lacey Private Beta — outbound v1

## Objective

Get qualified discovery, not sell a finished product. The first ask is 15 minutes. The strongest signal is willingness to walk through one completed historical shipment/entry.

## Core customs-broker message

**Subject:** Quick Lacey workflow question

Hi {{name/team}},

{{personalized_opening}}

I’m validating a document-intelligence workflow for Lacey Act entries. The idea is to take the commercial invoice, supplier spreadsheet and supporting files, extract the declaration inputs that are actually present, and flag missing or conflicting species, harvest-country, quantity and shipment data **before** the entry reaches the filing step.

I’m not trying to replace ACE/LAWGS or the customs broker. I’m looking for a few U.S. brokers willing to show me how one completed Lacey entry is handled today so I can measure where the manual work actually sits.

Would you be open to a 15-minute walkthrough? Client names/prices can be redacted and I do not need access to a live entry or your systems.

Best,
José
Litoral Trace

## Qualification rule after reply

A reply becomes **qualified** only if the person has direct exposure to at least one of:
- collecting Lacey inputs from importer/supplier;
- reviewing species/harvest-country/quantity/value data;
- preparing or filing entries;
- supplier legality/origin evidence;
- managing the supporting record set.

Do not count polite replies, generic sales referrals, or newsletter responses as qualified conversations.

# Litoral Trace V1 — Pricing

Status: commercial baseline for pilots and first customers. Prices are quoted in USD or the agreed ARS equivalent. Taxes, payment processor fees, and customer-specific integration work are not included unless stated in a proposal.

## Starter — USD 249/month

For a small exporter, supplier, or consultant beginning structured EUDR evidence management.

- up to 2 users
- up to 100 active lots
- secure XLSX ingestion
- lot geolocation and traceability
- Vault evidence management
- basic audit trail
- up to 50 satellite analyses/month
- evidence exports
- standard support

Recommended onboarding: USD 300–500 one time.

## Compliance — USD 499/month

Flagship package for companies operating an ongoing traceability/compliance workflow.

- up to 10 users
- up to 1,000 active lots
- XLSX batch ingestion within reasonable-use limits
- full Vault workflow
- RBAC and audit trail
- satellite analysis and batch evidence linkage
- operational dashboard and evidence views
- priority support

Recommended onboarding: USD 750–1,500 one time.

Annual reference price: USD 4,990 prepaid, equivalent to roughly two months of discount versus twelve monthly payments.

## Enterprise — from USD 990/month

For larger organizations or customers requiring greater volume, multiple organizational units, integrations, custom reporting, onboarding/training, or negotiated service levels.

Pricing is scoped after discovery. Typical initial range: USD 990–1,500/month, with higher tiers possible for materially higher volume or custom obligations.

Recommended onboarding: from USD 2,000.

## Volume and add-ons

The primary commercial scaling dimensions are lots/operations, documents/storage, satellite usage, organizational scope, integrations and service level — not seat count alone.

Reference Compliance overage bands:

- 1,001–2,500 active lots: +USD 150/month
- 2,501–5,000 active lots: +USD 300/month

Potential add-ons:

- Satellite Pack
- additional Vault storage
- additional organization/business unit
- API/integration package
- custom reports/training

## Commercial guardrails

Litoral Trace is sold as traceability and evidence infrastructure. It does not sell an “EUDR certificate”, does not represent itself as a regulator or certification body, and does not guarantee a legal compliance outcome. Customer-specific commitments must be written in the signed commercial proposal.

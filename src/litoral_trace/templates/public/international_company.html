Company · Argentina

Independent by design. Focused on evidence.

Litoral Trace is an Argentina-founded, founder-led software project serving U.S. importers and customs brokers who manage Lacey Act documentation.

What we build

Our initial commercial product helps teams organize original shipment documents, identify missing or inconsistent information, carry out explicit human review, preserve a decision trail, and reuse supplier evidence subject to verification controls.

We are a small, independently operated business. Our product should earn trust through transparent workflows and verifiable evidence—not by implying a large corporate team or affiliations we do not hold.

Who we serve

U.S. import compliance operations, customs brokers and importers handling wood and plant-product supply chains. We especially welcome conversations with businesses sourcing from Argentina and Latin America, provided the U.S. Lacey workflow is relevant to their entries.

Where we operate

Founded and independently operated from Argentina; focused on software for the U.S. market. No U.S. corporate registration, government affiliation or third-party certification is implied.

Accountability & product boundaries

The platform supports evidence-based preparation and review. It is not a customs broker, does not provide legal advice, does not submit U.S. government filings on behalf of customers, and does not certify that any shipment complies with law. Business users retain the duty to validate their information and filing decisions.
